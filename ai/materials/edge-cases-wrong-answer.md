Wrong Answer means the program ran and finished but printed something different from the expected output on at least one test. Check: negative numbers and zero; both numbers equal; the larger value being first or second; values at the limits; output format (one value per line, no extra text). Re-read the statement for conditions you assumed. For absolute difference remember |A-B| is never negative. For maximum, handle equal inputs and negative inputs.
